# GUI checklist for the movie maker

Sixteen blocks, one box each. Tick a block when everything listed in it works; when something does not, write what you saw under that block. The bullets say what to do and what to expect.

Everything was written without being able to run the GUI. Start from the example workspace `docs/examples/aspirin_phospholipase_movie.via` (put it next to `aspirin-phospholipase.gro`, `.xtc` and `.edr`, then **File > Open Workspace**). A second example, `docs/examples/aspirin_binding_movie.via`, uses the same files and shows a dolly zoom (16 to 24 s), bullet time (25 to 30.5 s) and representation hand-overs; it is worth a look after blocks 6 and 11 to 13. Open **Windows > Movie**: the timeline is on the left and the tabbed controls on the right. The manual is [`docs/movie_maker.md`](docs/movie_maker.md).

Already checked by you (blocks 1 to 5) are ticked.

## A. Editor, workspace, overlays (checked)

- [x] **1. Editor layout and workspace.** Divider, independent scrolling, hide **Timeline** / **Controls** (one always stays), shared playback and **Add Keyframe**; six tabs with their own controls; controls disabled while recording except Stop/Pause/Resume. Open the example (no log errors, 80 s, playhead at about 55.45 s); save under another name and reopen: keys, overlays, markers, representation keys, output settings and lanes are all back. Older workspaces (no representation ids, no overlays, keys from before the follow target, old one-figure plot overlays) load and convert.
- [x] **2. Overlays: text, time stamp, scale bar, logo, image, time bar.** A new movie has the logo (removing it sticks after save/open); transparent, sharp, readable on light and dark. Position, size (% or points, no jump when switching, same size in points at 1080p and 4K), color, background plate, fades, **Duplicate**, change **Type**, undo/redo all work. The scale bar follows zoom. The time stamp starts at 0, only grows (also backward) and shows time since the start when shown late. The image loads with transparency and jpg, path survives moving the workspace, a missing file turns red without a crash. The time bar fills forward whichever way the trajectory plays, with grey track and plate. **Add overlay...** offers every type; the list is compact.
- [x] **3. Overlays: timeline and distribution.** Subplots stacked with their own scale, legend and value; **Add subplot**, **Up**, **Remove**; **As the movie plays**, **Value**, **Titles**, **Markers**; elapsed vs trajectory axis, backward trajectory still grows to the right; late-appearing overlay already shows the past; subplots that come in at their own times (plate grows, others do not move); distribution counts frames played so far; **Number of bins** 32/37/128 and **Use source bin counts**; marker targets (one subplot vs **All timeline subplots**) and names survive save/load, undo, length change and renaming; look settings (points, colors, plate presets) are the same in a recording at another resolution.
- [x] **4. Overlays: property visualization and the frame.** A property overlay shows highlight, lines and labels in the viewport and in a recording, fades with the overlay and leaves nothing highlighted when it ends. In **Movie preview** the frame follows the resolution (also tall custom sizes); the recording matches it (camera, overlays, scale bar, labels, sharp at 1080p and at **Scale** 25 %, no smearing with **Samples per frame** > 1). Closing the Movie window or recording removes the dimming. Atom picking still hits the right atom.
- [x] **5. Camera keys basics.** **Add Keyframe** and **K** (on an existing key the new one goes 2 s later and the preview time follows); **Key on Selection** (also for a molecule split by the cell); **Copy** / **Paste Keyframe** / Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V; drag a row by its number; **Look at** (selection, picked atom, view aim, **Stop tracking**) tracks atoms through the trajectory; **Update position**; the look-at path in the viewport grows without stalling the UI and matches **Play Preview**.

## B. Camera (to check)

- [ ] **6. Aiming, orbits, loops, upright.** Double-click in the viewport aims at the clicked point with the eye fixed. **Add Orbit** with **Snap to frames** puts the end key where it should be and is refused past the movie end. **Close Loop** and **Seamless loop**: no jump on repeat. **Keep upright**: keys captured tilted play level, spins and loops never flip; **Up** and **From view** pick the axis; **Roll (deg)** tilts and eases, 0 levels; Go To, path cameras and Ctrl+click on the path agree with the preview. Off: **Roll** shows each tilt and **Level** straightens it. Undo/save/load keep it; an old workspace opens with it off.
- [ ] **7. Camera lane.** Orange dots with number and name (yellow at the preview time). Drag a dot (preview follows, list re-sorts on release, snaps to frames, Ctrl+Z undoes); click goes to the key; right-click: type a name (appears next to the number, in the **Name** column, survives save/open), **Go to**, **Remove** (Ctrl+Z restores). Double-click an empty spot adds a key on the path without moving the camera (not where a key is, not while recording). Rows **Camera keys**, **Look at** (blue band, "3 atoms" / "atom 12"), **Focus** (magenta band from each key that sets it to the next, transition darker), **Spin** (purple `+2 x`), **Frame** (green mark with the frame number); hover shows details; a key added between two that look at the same atoms looks at them too.
- [ ] **8. Camera path in the viewport.** Blue eye path, yellow look-at path, brighter ahead than behind. Green camera at the preview time (dot, ring with cross and **looks at**, arrow). Ticks at round times with readable labels and chevrons, crowding near an **Ease in/out** key; **Time ticks**, **Sight lines**, **Cameras**, **Spin rings** each switch off and are saved. Handles: numbered eye dot (with name) and ring with cross; white (fixed look-at) / blue (atoms) / orange; hover lights the key in the camera lane and vice versa; click goes to the key without selecting or rotating; drag the eye (the ring stays), drag the ring (the eye stays), Ctrl+drag both, Esc cancels, one undo step; works for keys that track atoms too (the look-at becomes a fixed point). A magenta handle on each key that sets the focus drags it as a fixed point. Ctrl+click on the path shows `add a key at ... s` and adds one without moving the camera. The path is not in recorded frames or screenshots and is hidden while recording and while a key waits for **Look at**. With depth of field on, the pink focus frame is still drawn.

## C. Timeline interaction (to check)

- [ ] **9. Picking, moving, stretching, keyboard.** Click, Ctrl+click and box pick (blue box; camera lane by time only; Ctrl/Shift adds; background click puts all down; the playhead and blue anchors still drag normally). A camera key's ring shows in every lane it is drawn in and around its viewport handle. Group drag in time keeps spacing, stops at the movie ends as a whole, snaps, tooltip `N keys: +x s`, one undo. Group drag in value (trajectory frames, distance, FOV, look parameter, representation property; limits respected; colors and **Visible** move in time only). A moved key landing on another replaces it on release. Bars and blocks: click/Ctrl+click/box pick; dragging by the middle moves everything picked, timed subplot times move with a bar, a block stops at other blocks of its representation, dragging an end changes only that end; Delete removes picked blocks and keys but not overlays. Stretch box (200 %, 50 %, about first item vs about preview time, stops at the movie end, one undo, back to 100 % on release). Inspector line (counts, **first at**, ease for all picked or **(mixed)**, rename one camera key). Keyboard: arrows (Shift second, Ctrl ten frames), up/down values, Delete, Esc, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C/V; buttons **Pick all**, **Put down**, **Delete**, **Copy**, **Paste** do the same; without picked keys arrows and Ctrl+C/V behave as before.
- [ ] **10. Ruler, timing, zoom, lanes.** Ruler: seconds, a tick per frame when zoomed (numbers follow zoom), markers as yellow triangles (hover time, click goes there), click/drag moves the preview time (snapped), red recording time while recording. Every lane shows its name. **Snap to frames** on: keys, parameter and representation keys, blue anchors and the playhead land on frames; off: free. Blue **Start** / **End** anchors (trajectory waits, then holds). **Movie length (s)** doubled: same pacing at half speed, overlays and fades scale, undo restores. Frame pin dragged up/down changes the frame. **Lens** lane: FOV on the left axis, distance on the right, legend; distance dot keeps looking at the same point, FOV dot changes the angle; box pick works on both curves. Ctrl+wheel zoom, middle button / Shift+wheel / sideways pan, wheel scrolls, **Show whole movie**. **Lane height** (tall lanes, scrolling, long lanes grow only themselves, divider drags resize two lanes), **Fit to window**. **Lanes** toggles hide each lane (lit when on, all hidden says so), presets **All** / **Camera work** / **Scene work**, toggles in the order the lanes are drawn (Ruler, Camera, Trajectory, Lens, Representations, Overlays, Rep keys, Look) on one line, **All** / **Camera work** / **Scene work** / **Layout...** on the line below, **Layout...** popup holds height, fit, snap, whole movie, Lines and Block height (greyed with Fit to window, saved); short axis numbers (no long decimals on axes or in the mouse readout); the Look / Rep keys pickers appear only with those lanes; lanes, height and **Fit to window** survive save/open. Overlay lane bars: drag, drag an end, subplot times move, ticks and markers, snapping, undo. Clicking a key's orange marker in the **Timelines** window goes to the key. Backward trajectory: frame pins with a lower second frame play backward and slow to a stop at the turn; **Trajectory Frames** with start above end plays backward in preview and recording.

## D. Scene view (to check)

- [ ] **11. Scene view, Movie preview, live picture, Preview.** Green **Preview** next to the switch: windows, menu, axes widget and camera path disappear, black outside the frame, the movie plays from the preview time (from 0 at the end); bottom bar play/pause, back to start, slider (scrub pauses, release resumes), **Repeat**, **Exit preview**; bar hides when the mouse is still during playback and comes back on move; Space plays/pauses (does not start the Animation window playback; with the Movie window up and not in Preview, Space also plays/pauses the movie, not the trajectory, and types a space in a text field; opening the Movie window while the trajectory plays stops it; closing the window while the movie plays stops it and Space plays the trajectory again; Space with the Density Volume window focused plays the movie, not both), Esc leaves; leaving restores the windows and Scene view if it was shown; disabled while recording. First **Switch to Scene view** frames the whole path from your direction, frame and overlays gone; **Fit path** frames it again, close (the path fills most of the free part). In Scene view, rotating turns around the middle of the free part (the path does not slide behind the window), the wheel zooms towards the mouse; in Movie preview the wheel zooms as before. With **Animate camera**, scrubbing and playing move the green camera and play the trajectory but not the viewport; clicking a key (lane, table **Go To**, handle) moves only the playhead and green camera. **Switch to Movie preview** returns to the movie camera (at the preview time with **Animate camera**, else the view you had), and back again to your Scene view pose. Tab and hold-Tab (over a third of a second) peek and return; Tab does nothing in a text field or while recording. **Show path** (top row) has its own state in each mode; in Movie preview the path is hidden unless it is ticked there. In Movie preview the frame sits in the part of the viewport the Movie window leaves free (window docked at the bottom: frame above it; at the right: left of it), the picture in it is the same as before (compare with a recorded frame), picking, path handles, overlays and property labels line up; moving the window moves the frame; a window floating in the middle keeps the frame centred. **Fit path** frames the path beside the window. In Scene view: the same frame as Movie preview (same size and place, outside dimmed, no overlays); **K** / **Add Keyframe** keys what the frame shows (switch to Movie preview at that time: same picture); right click **Add Keyframe** > **Add a key on the path** adds one on the path without changing its shape; **Update position** works; **Key on Selection** frames the selection from the view's direction. Recording from Scene view uses the movie camera and returns to the Scene view pose. **Live picture** (lower right of the Movie window, with overlays, movie proportions, absent in Movie preview) follows scrubbing, playing, handle drags and FOV changes; right-click: Small / Medium / Large / Extra large / Hide (sizes grow with the window width) (unticks **Live picture**); the main viewport is unchanged by it (no flicker or ghosting with TAA, picking and selection still right, depth of field and occlusion same as with **Live picture** off); follow keys show the right frame; nothing is rendered for it with the window closed or collapsed; frame rate with **Live picture** on is acceptable on a large system.

## E. Representations and looks (to check)

- [ ] **23. Movie undo keyboard focus.** Edit a camera key or overlay, then Ctrl+Z with the Movie window active and with the viewport active: one movie edit is undone. Ctrl+Y/Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes one edit from either surface. Typing in a text field keeps text undo instead of movie undo; recording and fullscreen Preview do not accept movie undo shortcuts.

- [ ] **22. Movie defaults and keyboard.** Fresh/reset movie: Fit to window enabled, All shows Ruler/Camera/Trajectory/Lens/Representations/Look, Look selects Depth of field blur, and with trajectory frame is checked. Camera work shows Ruler/Camera/Trajectory/Lens; Scene work shows Ruler/Trajectory/Representations/Overlays/Look and selects blur. Individual Overlays/Rep keys toggles still work; saved workspace layouts override defaults. With nonzero viewport blur, open Movie: unkeyed movie blur is zero, Scene retains viewport blur; keyed movie blur still plays, and closing restores viewport blur. Reopen and repeat after recording. Tab and hold-Tab switch/peek with the Movie window active, not while typing or recording.

- [ ] **12. Representation keys, overview, swap.** **Visible** keys grow in / shrink away over **Transition (s)** (2 s default; 0 instant; 10 slow; two keys closer than the transition turn around); cartoon/ribbon look right at tiny sizes; a dipole or electronic-structure representation stays full size and vanishes at the end. Scales, **Saturation**, **Tint scale**, **Tint color**, **Base color** (Uniform mapping) key and scrub correctly (the example shrinks the ligand's ball scale 30 s to 38 s). Overview: one row per system (`protein-cartoon`, `protein-cpk`, `protein-vdw` and plain `protein` share a row; renaming regroups), labelled colored blocks, unkeyed enabled representation covers the whole movie, blocks cross-fade, systems separated by gap, divider and shading, **Layout... > Lines** Compact / Stacked / Separate (Separate: one line per representation, grouped by system, blocks stay on their representation's line when dragged; saved with the workspace); **Block height** makes blocks taller or flatter and the lane follows. Edit: drag block or edge (neighbours of the same representation constrain), double-click empty space adds, right-click **Remove block** or switch (CPK to VDW interval transfers, overlapping blocks merge, other systems and property keys intact), removing the last block hides it, undo/redo for each, example groups intact. **Swap with the next of its group** works (disabled with one member, undo). Representation lane: pick representation and property, drag in time and value, double-click adds, right-click removes, **Visible** double-click turns around and keys time 0 the first time. Duplicate a keyed representation (copy has no keys), remove one (keys gone). Save/open keeps ids and keys. After recording or **Animate parameters** off the representations are as before; the **Representations** window is locked while recording.
- [ ] **13. Look parameters and depth of field.** Key a parameter at two times and scrub; drag in the lane, double-click adds, right-click removes; **Animate parameters** off lets go of parameters and representations. Movie **Depth of field blur** above zero enables blur without the global switch; zero sharpens everything. Legacy global focus-distance keys log that they are inactive.
- [ ] **19. Viewport/movie focus separation.** Global focus is always Auto: double-click sets the look-at/focus point, with no Fixed control. Open Movie: global controls show driven by movie and cannot be edited; Scene view keeps its own internal depth while the live picture follows movie focus. Switch Scene/Movie preview, play fullscreen, record, then close Movie: Auto and saved blur return. Legacy global fixed/target modes report conversion to Auto on load without changing movie keys.
- [ ] **20. Look at and Focus on per key.** Table order after FOV is Go to, Update, Look at, Focus on, Ease, Spin, Roll, Copy, Dup, Remove; each action works. Shift+K captures from the Movie window, viewport and fullscreen Preview, but not while typing or recording. Look at: Fixed point / Track selection; edit coordinates or use view aim; switching from Track to Fixed freezes the live centre and keeps the eye still. Track uses selected atoms, or starts picking when none are selected; typed expressions also work. Focus on: Look-at / Track selection; after a key tracking B, a default key aimed at C focuses at C, not B. Legacy point/distance focus remains unchanged on opening the popup. Transition/Ease finishes before the next camera key and reproduces while scrubbing both ways. Verify periodic groups, recentering/orientation correction, invalid selection errors, save/open, undo/redo and group copy/paste/stretch. Blue/yellow/magenta path handles edit their own target, Ctrl moves eye and aim, Esc cancels. Live picture, Movie preview, fullscreen Preview and recording agree; Spin, Keep upright and Roll remain correct. Open the optics tutorial: carbon at 18 s, fixed red oxygen at 32 s, blur fades off at 34 s.
- [ ] **21. Full-width keys and Lens distance.** Keyframe table stays in the bottom pane on every tab, across the whole width. Resize the horizontal divider; upper timeline gains width and height, both panes scroll, and the divider survives save/open. Check small windows and recording-disabled controls. Live picture never covers the table. Lens distance points appear on the right axis for fixed and tracked aim, line up with the curve, support box/group selection and unit conversion, and drag the eye without changing look-at or focus targets; undo/redo restores them.

## F. Output (to check)

- [ ] **14. Recording.** **PNG sequence** (numbered from 0, view and playback restored afterwards); **MP4 H.264** plays; **MP4 H.265** and **WebM VP9** (needs libx265 / libvpx-vp9) play, have the right extension in the log and workspace copy, and the **Quality (CRF)** range changes (0 to 63 for VP9). **Pause** / **Resume** in the window and on the banner: no duplicated or skipped frame. Time left appears after a few frames and is about right. **Scale** 25 %: small even-sized frames, fast, overlays scale. **Samples per frame**: 1 fast and rough, 8+ smooth. **Render only a range** 2 to 4 s: PNG keeps whole-movie numbers (frame 48 is `..._00048.png`), MP4 is 2 s. **Save a workspace copy with the movie** writes `prefix.via` without changing the open workspace's name; opening it and recording gives the same movie. Esc and **Stop Recording** keep the frames so far.

## G. Overlay placement (to check)

- [ ] **16. Overlays and markers do not collide.** A time bar at bottom left and a wide timeline at bottom centre shown at the same time: the time bar sits above the timeline's plate, in preview and recording; shown at different times, neither moves. Two overlays in the same top corner stack downwards. A top-right distribution with a tall middle-right timeline (`aspirin_binding_movie.via`, 40 to 58 s): the distribution stays in its corner and the timeline moves down a little. No overlay is ever pushed out of the frame. Positions do not jump while the movie plays. Two markers close in time on one subplot: the second label goes on a lower row instead of overlapping. A marker in the second half of a timeline: its label ends at the marker line (runs left), one in the first half starts at it; no label runs past the plot. Close markers in the editor's ruler: labels on separate lines, none over the frame numbers. Several markers: each has its own color, the same for its line and label in the overlay and its triangles in the ruler and overlay lane; the swatch picks one, right click returns to automatic; saved and loaded; undo restores a color change.
- [ ] **17. Panel reorganisation.** New movie is 60 s with the trajectory spread over it. No **Animate camera**: **Play Preview** and the green **Preview** move the camera along the keys in Movie preview (Scene view keeps your camera). **Play from start** next to **Play Preview**; the scrubber is narrower. On the right: **Undo** / **Redo**, then the **Keyframes** section (**Add Keyframe** with right-click **Add a key on the path**, **with trajectory frame**, **Key on Selection**, follow target), then the tabs; **Start Recording** at the bottom of **Output**; **Stop** / **Pause** / progress at the top while recording. **K** or **Add Keyframe** on an existing key adds one 2 s later and moves the preview time; refused past the end. Keyframe table: icon buttons Go to / Look at (highlighted while picking) / Update position, and **⋯** with Follow, Duplicate, Copy, Remove; old workspaces with `AnimateCamera` still load.
- [ ] **18. Preview from start, roll from view, hold shading.** Green **Preview** starts at 0 whatever the preview time. With **Keep upright** on, tilt the view with the trackball and press **K**: the key's Roll takes the tilt and **Play Preview** shows the shot as keyed (no flip or straightening); **Update position** does the same. The Camera lane is hatched grey before the first and after the last key, with a tooltip.
